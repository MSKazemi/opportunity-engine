# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith. Criticise code, not people.

## Standards

Behaviour that helps: clear reports, patience with newcomers, accepting that a maintainer may say
no, and disagreeing about a technical decision without making it personal.

Behaviour that is not welcome: harassment of any kind, personal or political attacks, publishing
someone's private information, sexualised language or imagery, and sustained disruption of
discussions.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and anywhere someone is
representing the project.

## A note specific to this project

This tool touches people's job searches, which means issues and discussions may involve someone's
employment situation, immigration status, health, gaps in a CV, or a rejection. Treat anything a
person shares about their own circumstances as theirs. Do not speculate about it, do not repeat it
elsewhere, and do not offer opinions about someone's eligibility or worth as a candidate.

Never post someone else's CV, profile, or application material in an issue. If a bug report needs
example data, redact it first — the maintainer will never ask for unredacted personal data, and any
message claiming to be the maintainer and asking for it is not.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to **mohsen.seyedkazemi@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately. The maintainer
may edit, hide or delete contributions, and may block accounts, for behaviour judged to breach this
document. Enforcement decisions are the maintainer's to make; if you think one was wrong, say so by
email and it will be reconsidered once.
